# LIMIT privacy data inventory — release-review draft

This inventory describes repository-visible flows as reviewed September 15, 2026. It is **not a privacy policy, legal approval, a complete vendor audit, or a submitted App Store privacy label**. Business identity, operating regions, legal bases, retention periods, vendor processing terms and generated-native-package behavior still require verification. Do not promise immediate destruction of all backups or uploaded files without proving the platform's actual behavior.

Apple's privacy disclosures cover the application and integrated third-party partners. Collection for app functionality still needs assessment; data linked to an account must not be described as anonymous just because it lacks a name. Review labels whenever the implementation or provider behavior changes. [Apple privacy-label guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

## Providers and purposes

- **Base44:** hosting, authentication, database, private files, backend functions and AI integration. The repository does not identify the complete downstream model-provider/subprocessor chain. Confirm current contracts, subprocessors, processing locations, retention and whether prompts/images may be retained or used for model improvement.
- **Identity providers:** Google is present in sign-in UI. Verify the enabled provider configuration and the native equivalent-login requirement. Do not claim Apple login exists until the full flow works in the package.
- **Apple / Android health repositories:** The web source defines an installed-app bridge and normalized storage for user-authorized Apple Health or Health Connect data. The generated native bridge is not present or verified here. The browser cannot read these stores. Confirm actual permissions, declarations, deletion and background behavior before claiming sync is available.
- **Watch, ring and scale providers:** Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Fitbit and Withings names can identify a data source; their presence in a source enum does not mean a direct account connection or data transfer exists. Direct provider OAuth, server-side token storage and provider-specific background/webhook adapters are not implemented in this repository.
- **Stripe:** dependency presence alone does not establish an active payment flow or data collection. There is no implemented commercial flow to disclose as a completed feature; reassess before connecting any payment service.

No advertising SDK, contact-book access, precise-location feature or microphone recording was identified in the application source inspected. This is not a claim that platform telemetry is absent. Do not select “data not collected” or “not used for tracking” solely from this inventory; inspect the generated wrapper and production service behavior.

## Health controls and deletion scope

Health settings let a member hide metrics from health summaries, choose a preferred source per metric, inspect saved connections/import history, and remove health-hub records without deleting the account. All controls are optional. Automatic source selection remains the default; display preferences do not stop an authorized bridge from importing other supported metrics.

The health-only removal flow requires an explicit confirmation and deletes the signed-in member's `HealthMetric`, `DailyCheckIn`, `HealthConnection`, `HealthImport`, `WeightEntry`, and legacy `BodyMeasurement` records. It checks ownership, removes paginated rows, and checks each collection again before reporting success. It preserves workouts, nutrition, `UserProfile` (including starting weight and goals), and `HealthPreference`. Do not describe it as deletion of every health-related datum in the account.

Health-only deletion does not remove records from Apple Health, Health Connect, provider accounts, existing exports, or vendor backups/logs, and does not itself revoke device permissions. Users must revoke access on their connected devices to prevent later reimports. Optional bridge disconnection and record deletion are separate operations. Within one running app, mutations are serialized and account changes cancel delayed operations; other devices can still write concurrently. Partial failure is shown as a retry, not successful complete erasure.

Full account export and account erasure use the shared inventory in `base44/shared/accountData.js`, including all five health collections, `WeightEntry`, and `BodyMeasurement`. The exported scope explicitly excludes original uploaded files, service logs and AI-provider records. Confirm authentication deletion, file retention, backups and downstream recipients separately before publishing broader erasure promises.

## Native-package review

The generated IPA may contain SDKs not listed in the web `package.json`. Inspect its privacy manifests, required-reason API declarations, entitlements, purpose strings and the privacy report from the real distribution archive. App Store privacy labels and native privacy manifests are separate artifacts. Do not manufacture required-reason codes or assert that a web-source scan covers the native shell. [Apple privacy-manifest documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy-manifest-files)

Camera/photo purpose text should explain the user's selected food-label or meal-photo workflow. Test denial and cancellation, and check that uploads happen only after an intentional action. HealthKit or Health Connect permissions should be added only when the generated native bridge exists, only for supported metrics, and only after an in-context user action. Location, contacts or microphone permissions should not be added for nonexistent features.

## Required evidence before approving a policy or labels

1. Map each data type to exact recipients, purpose, account linkage, remote retention and deletion behavior; verify the private upload and model-provider paths with disposable data.
2. Resolve account deletion across database rows, authentication identities, uploaded files, logs and backups. Document any lawful retention and its duration accurately; verify retries and partial failure.
3. Confirm AI consent precedes sending photos, source documents or personalized context, remains understandable, and does not prevent manual logging or editing when declined.
4. Verify live account isolation with two ordinary accounts, including private-file signing and server-owned records. A schema rule is not evidence of the deployed permissions.
5. Verify the actual business/publisher identity, support contact, operating regions, age audience, vendors and legal basis with the responsible owner/reviewer. Do not fill these with guesses.
6. Reconcile this inventory with the approved public documents, deployed application, generated IPA and App Store Connect answers; attach the evidence to `release/app-store.json`.

Privacy and sensitive-data handling are release gates, not marketing copy. Keep reports free of real customer data and signing credentials.
